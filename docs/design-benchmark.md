identify the structural decisions that make strong startup sites feel clear,

## Direction selected

**Editorial diagnostic studio** — warm ivory paper, near-black ink, one electric
green signal color, cobalt for information, an expressive serif for the large
claims, and compact sans-serif UI type everywhere else.

The system follows five rules:

1. One job per screen and one dominant CTA per page.
2. Evidence appears before broad claims.
3. Photography documents a real six-person team; it never decorates empty space.
4. Motion reveals sequence or causality and respects reduced-motion settings.
5. Prices, deliverables, process, and limitations stay visible.
